Noise Meter - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

About the App

Noise Meter uses your device microphone to measure ambient sound level in real time and shows the result in dB.

Data We Process

  • Microphone access: used only to calculate real-time sound level.
  • Advertising data: the app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The SDK may process identifiers, ad interaction, and diagnostic data.

Why We Use Data

  • To measure and display ambient noise level
  • To show in-app ads
  • To support ad delivery, security, and performance

Data Sharing

  • We do not upload or store microphone audio recordings on our servers.
  • Data processed by AdMob is subject to Google's own policies.

Data Retention

  • The app does not keep permanent audio recordings.
  • Temporary technical data may be stored on-device for app operation.

Permissions

  • RECORD_AUDIO: required for noise measurement.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: required to load and manage ad content.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: may be used for advertising identifier related operations.

Children's Privacy

This app is not specifically directed to children.
