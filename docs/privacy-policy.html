Terms of Use

Noise Meter is provided for informational and general reference purposes. Measurement values may vary depending on device hardware, microphone quality, operating system processing, user distance from the sound source, and background noise conditions.

The app is not a certified industrial, medical, legal, occupational safety, or compliance-grade measurement instrument. You should not rely on this app alone where official or high-risk measurements are required.

Privacy Policy

Noise Meter uses microphone permission only to calculate and display sound level values on the device. Audio is processed locally for measurement and is not intentionally recorded, uploaded, or stored on our servers.

The app does not require account registration and does not request personally identifying profile data for core functionality. If third-party platform or advertising services are used, their own policies may apply according to their documentation and applicable regulations.
